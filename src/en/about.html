Our Mission

"To make rigorous mathematical methods usable for operations teams. Solid models, validated on real data, replacing guesswork in day-to-day decisions."

Lontra was founded by Juan Pablo Morandé, who saw that real operational problems — from berth allocation to freight pricing — outpace what off-the-shelf software can model. We build optimization and simulation systems tailored to each client's actual constraints.

The Lontra Method

Our Methodology

Data-driven decisions, validated in real operations.
